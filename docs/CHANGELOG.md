### Changed

- TypeScript stays on 6.x (Dependabot ignores majors) until typescript-eslint and `@astrojs/check` support 7.
- Reviewer subagent runs on a different model (Sonnet) from the builder.
- Removed a redundant `Write` deny rule for the legacy repository from `.claude/settings.json` (the `Edit` rule covers it).
- Phase 3 gate requests: the logo wordmark renders at a legible size (cause of the "grainy" logo: the vector SVG was drawn too small, not a raster), with the 300 px minimum now measured on the visible artwork (Q-23) and 400 px of artwork in the header and footer from 64rem; the Maps link opens the renamed Google Business Profile listing (pin `-15.8463737, -47.8944906`; Q-03 resolved); the contact heading reads "Contato"; neutral wording replaces four self-descriptive practice-area phrases; the mobile header keeps the "Menu" button on a second row because it does not fit beside the logo at 360 px (Q-24); before and after screenshots were reviewed at the gate and not kept in the repository.
- Phase 4 gate requests: the privacy policy states one purpose per data category, a retention criterion for contact messages, processing outside Brazil by Cloudflare, Google and WhatsApp (Meta), the e-mail as the data subject channel and the controller's CNPJ (stored once in `src/config/site.ts`); the terms add an item on the edition date of publications; the two 2026 booklets show a neutral note on the period they refer to; "críticas" and "críticos" dropped from the election booklet topics; legal basis and "sem aviso prévio" left to the lawyer review (Q-26).
- Phase 5 follow-up: the Cloudflare Web Analytics token of the existing `mmec.adv.br` site is set (Q-29), so the beacon and its two CSP hosts are live; Phase 8 steps collected in `docs/launch-checklist.md`. Lazy images get an explicit `aspect-ratio` from their `width` and `height` attributes (`src/styles/base.css`): Chromium flagged the publication covers, the home watermark and the footer logo as "Lazy-loaded images should have explicit dimensions" because `height: auto` overrode the attributes they already had; `tests/images.spec.ts` checks the attributes, the absence of that issue and that no image is distorted. The beacon is added by a same-origin script only on `mmec.adv.br` and `www.mmec.adv.br`, since Cloudflare's CORS policy rejected its report from `astro preview` (Lighthouse failed best practices on the console errors) and from the preview Worker; it is validated in production at the cutover. `tests/analytics.spec.ts` covers local, preview and production hosts.
- Phase 7 fix: the privacy policy showed "e-mailcontato@…" and "Brasília/DF,CEP" because Astro drops whitespace at a line break before an element or expression.
- Phase 6 fixes: logos stay visible in forced-colours mode (ink file, on cream under a dark theme); print drops the button groups and no longer repeats e-mail addresses; the first cover on `/publicacoes` loads eagerly with high priority.
